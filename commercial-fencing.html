Commercial Fencing

Commercial Fencing in Albuquerque, NM

A commercial fence is specified for the traffic on that site: heavier chain link, ornamental metal, and gates a vehicle or a crew can use. It is not a backyard fence with a business name on the invoice.

Request an estimate Call (505) 210-9903

When the fence should be a commercial spec

Choose commercial chain link or ornamental metal when the line has to take steady traffic, mark a yard, or close a site. The gate is part of the spec, because that is the opening people actually use.

A barbed or angled top is a site decision for a commercial line, not the default on a house. We do not put that treatment on a residential backyard.

Where this fence fits local lots

Choose a commercial spec for a lot, a storage yard, or a job site. Height, gauge, and the gates follow that use, not a house catalog.

Materials and styles

The estimate names the height, style, gate count, and anything on the line that changes the dig: slope, caliche, trees, and tear-out. We do not publish a flat price per foot.

Repair versus a new run

Sound posts can take a repair. Posts that have failed at the ground usually mean a new line is the clearer job. We will say which one it is after we see the fence.

What an estimate covers

Length, height, style, gates, post method, access, and removal of any existing fence that has to come out. Call (505) 210-9903 or use the form with the town and a rough foot count.

How a commercial fence is specified

Use, height, and openings come before a price per foot.

  1. 1

    Name the use

    Storage yard, parking edge, job site, or a frontage that should stay open and ornamental. The use picks the material.

  2. 2

    Set the openings

    Walk gates, drive gates, and how wide a truck is. The gate posts are not line posts.

  3. 3

    Match the spec

    Height, gauge, coating, and whether a top treatment belongs on that site. A house gauge is the wrong default.

  4. 4

    Build the line

    Terminal posts, fabric or ornamental sections, and gates. We leave the gates swinging, not tied shut.

What changes a commercial price

Heavier material and wider gates are the difference from a backyard number.

Height and gauge

Taller fabric and heavier pipe cost more than a light residential chain-link fence of the same length.

Gates

A drive gate for trucks is a large share of a commercial job. Count the openings before you compare footage rates.

Top treatment

An angled top or barbed wire, where the site calls for it, is material and labor. It is quoted only when you ask for that spec.

Ground and access

Caliche, pavement, and a site that is still under construction change how posts go in.

Commercial fence questions

How much does commercial fencing cost?

It is priced from the length, the height, the gauge or the ornamental weight, and the gates. A drive gate can matter as much as a long run of fabric. Ground that is paved or full of caliche changes the posts. We do not publish a commercial rate per foot, because the spec is the price.

Can you fence a storage yard or a job site?

Yes. Those lines are usually chain link, with gates sized for the vehicles that use them. A job site and a permanent yard are not always the same spec. Tell us which one it is, and whether the fence has to come back out. That changes posts and pricing.

Do you put barbed wire on residential fences?

No. An angled top or barbed wire is a commercial site spec when the property calls for it. We do not put that on a house or a backyard. If a photo on this page shows a security top, that is the commercial product, not the residential one.

What materials do you use on a commercial line?

Chain link, in galvanized or a color coating, and ornamental steel or aluminum where the frontage should stay open. The gauge, the height, and the gates are written on the estimate. We do not substitute a light backyard fence and call it commercial.

Can you add a gate a truck can use?

Yes. Measure the largest vehicle and the swing or the slide you have room for. The posts for that leaf are part of the price. A walk gate beside it is a second opening. Tell us both if the crew and the trucks use different doors.

Is a commercial fence the same product as a backyard fence?

No. The pipe, the fabric, and the gates are sized for different traffic. A residential chain-link price is not a bid for a storage yard. If you have both a house and a lot, they can be two lines on one visit. They are still two specs.

Talk through the line

Call or send the lot details. We will tell you if the job is a repair, a replacement, or a different material than you started with.
