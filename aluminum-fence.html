Aluminum Fence

Aluminum Fence in Albuquerque, NM

Aluminum is the open fence: it marks the yard, keeps a view, and is the material we use most often around pools. It is not a privacy wall, and it can dent.

Request an estimate Call (505) 210-9903

When aluminum is the right fence

Choose aluminum when you want the space enclosed and the view left alone. Pool yards are the clearest case, because you can see the water and the metal does not rust the way steel does around chlorine and splash.

Skip it when you need a solid screen, or where cars and mowers will hit a thin picket. A dent is the real downside of a light ornamental fence. A heavier steel line is a different product, on its own page.

Where this fence fits local lots

Choose aluminum when the fence should stay open, including around a pool. Sections can follow a slope more easily than a tall privacy panel.

Materials and styles

The estimate names the height, style, gate count, and anything on the line that changes the dig: slope, caliche, trees, and tear-out. We do not publish a flat price per foot.

Repair versus a new run

Sound posts can take a repair. Posts that have failed at the ground usually mean a new line is the clearer job. We will say which one it is after we see the fence.

What an estimate covers

Length, height, style, gates, post method, access, and removal of any existing fence that has to come out. Call (505) 210-9903 or use the form with the town and a rough foot count.

How an aluminum install goes

The gate and the slope matter as much as the picket style.

  1. 1

    Name the job

    Pool, front yard, or a side line that should stay open. A pool fence is priced as a barrier, not as short decoration.

  2. 2

    Walk the slope

    Sections can rack down a hill. A gate still needs a swing that will not bind, so the gate spot is chosen on site.

  3. 3

    Pick the finish

    Black ornamental is the common request. The estimate says aluminum, not a vague metal fence.

  4. 4

    Set and latch

    Posts, sections, and gates go in together. On a pool, the latch and the swing are part of the job.

What changes an aluminum price

Ornamental aluminum is often less upkeep than steel. It is not automatically the cheapest fence we install. Chain link usually wins that comparison.

Style

A simple picket and a taller ornamental profile do not cost the same. Heavier rails add material.

Pool hardware

A pool gate and latch are specified differently from a garden gate. Say it is a pool on the first call.

Grade

Racking sections to a slope, or stepping them, changes posts and cuts. Steep spots take longer.

Gates

Each opening is hardware. If vehicles swing into the line, we may tell you this light profile is the wrong metal.

Aluminum fence questions

Is aluminum a good fence around a pool?

It is the fence we usually recommend for a pool because you can see through it and it does not rust the way steel does around water. A pool fence is also a barrier, so the gate, the latch, and the height have to suit the rules for that locality. Do not price it as a short decorative picket and hope it qualifies. Tell us it is a pool fence on the first call.

Will an aluminum fence rust in Albuquerque?

Aluminum does not rust the way steel does. It can still chalk, scratch, or fade a little in strong sun. Irrigation water and pool splash are not a reason to avoid it. If someone is selling you aluminum mainly as a salt-air product, that pitch does not describe this climate.

Can aluminum follow a sloped yard?

Open aluminum sections can often rack to follow a slope, which a tall privacy panel usually cannot do without stepping. A gate still needs a flat enough swing that it will latch. We pick the gate spot on the walk-through. The slope changes the look and the labor.

How is aluminum different from a steel fence?

Aluminum is lighter and does not rust the way bare steel does. Steel is heavier and depends on its coating. Aluminum dents more easily if a mower or a bumper hits it. They are different estimates, which is why steel ornamental fence has its own page.

Can a thin aluminum picket stand up to the wind?

An open picket lets wind through, which is an advantage over a solid privacy panel on an exposed lot. The posts still have to be set for the height and the gate. Wind is not a reason to skip aluminum. A dent from impact is the downside to plan for.

When should I skip aluminum?

Skip it when you need a solid screen, when the line will take vehicle hits, or when the job is a pasture. Aluminum is an open yard and pool fence. It is not a horse fence and it is not a privacy wall.
