Picket Fence

Picket Fencing in Albuquerque, NM

A picket fence is an open border in wood or vinyl. It marks a front yard and leaves the house visible. It is not the fence you choose when the goal is to block a view.

Request an estimate Call (505) 210-9903

When a picket fence is the right job

Choose picket for a front or side line where you want a boundary without a solid wall. Spacing, height, and capped posts change the look more than the street name does.

Skip it when the goal is privacy, a dog that can squeeze through wide gaps, or a commercial lot. A backyard screen is wood or vinyl privacy. A dog run is usually chain link or a tighter build.

Where this fence fits local lots

Choose picket when the yard should stay open. Wood can be painted or stained. Vinyl skips that coat. Neither one is a privacy wall.

Materials and styles

The estimate names the height, style, gate count, and anything on the line that changes the dig: slope, caliche, trees, and tear-out. We do not publish a flat price per foot.

Repair versus a new run

Sound posts can take a repair. Posts that have failed at the ground usually mean a new line is the clearer job. We will say which one it is after we see the fence.

What an estimate covers

Length, height, style, gates, post method, access, and removal of any existing fence that has to come out. Call (505) 210-9903 or use the form with the town and a rough foot count.

How a picket install goes

The material and the spacing are the job. The gate has to match them.

  1. 1

    Pick wood or vinyl

    Wood can be painted to the house. Vinyl is the version you do not repaint. The estimate names one of them.

  2. 2

    Set the spacing

    Wider gaps look lighter and contain less. Tighter pickets cost more material. We do not guess the gap from a catalog photo.

  3. 3

    Check the front yard

    Front yards and corner lots are where height rules usually show up. We do not print a citywide height, because the parcel decides it.

  4. 4

    Hang a matching gate

    The walk gate uses the same picket and the same hardware story. A mismatched gate is what makes a new fence look unfinished.

What changes a picket price

Short front-yard runs are still real jobs. The gate can be a large share of a small line.

Material

Wood picket and vinyl picket are different prices and different upkeep. Paint is not included unless the estimate says so.

Height and spacing

Taller pickets and tighter spacing use more material. A low, open picket is the smaller build.

Gates

On a short front line, one gate can move the price more than another ten feet of fence.

Access and grade

A sidewalk edge, a slope, and holes in caliche still take time on a fence that looks simple.

Picket fence questions

How much does a picket fence cost?

It is priced by the foot, then by wood versus vinyl, the height, the spacing, and the gate. A short front yard can still cost more than the footage suggests, because the gate is a large share of a small job. We do not publish a rate. The line and the material have to be named.

Can a picket fence give me privacy?

No. A picket fence is an open border. People can see through it, which is the point on a front yard. If you want the backyard screened, price a wood or vinyl privacy fence. Buying a taller picket and hoping it becomes a wall is the wrong product.

Should a picket fence be wood or vinyl?

Wood is the one you can paint or stain to the trim. Vinyl is the one you do not coat every few years, and strong sun can still fade a dark color. The look is similar from the street. The upkeep is not. Tell us which maintenance you actually want.

Will a picket fence work in a front yard here?

Often, if the height and the placement fit that parcel. Many Albuquerque streets already use walls or no fence at all, so a picket is a choice, not the default. Corner lots and the front setback are where rules usually change. Check the office that covers the property before we dig.

Can the spacing change for a dog?

Wider picket gaps will not hold a small dog. Tighter spacing uses more pickets and costs more. Even then, a picket fence is a poor dig barrier compared with chain link that stays close to the ground. Tell us the animal before we call a picket the right fence.

Can you repair a picket fence without replacing the posts?

Yes, when the posts are solid and the damage is pickets, a rail, or the gate. Rotted posts at the soil line mean the new pickets will lean with them. We check the posts first. A few broken pickets are a repair. A soft line is a rebuild.

Talk through the line

Call or send the lot details. We will tell you if the job is a repair, a replacement, or a different material than you started with.
