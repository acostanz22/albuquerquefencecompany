Chain-link Fence

Chain Link Fence in Albuquerque, NM

Chain link is the practical enclosure: a yard, a dog run, or a commercial lot. It does not pretend to be a privacy wall unless you add slats or a screen.

Request an estimate Call (505) 210-9903

When chain link is the right fence

Choose it when you need a boundary that is sturdy and see-through, and when privacy is not the point. Homes use it for dogs and side yards. Businesses use a heavier version for lots and storage.

Skip bare chain link if the goal is to block a view. Slats or a windscreen can help, and they also catch wind, which matters on an open West Side or mesa lot. A wood or vinyl privacy fence is the direct way to screen a backyard.

Where this fence fits local lots

Choose chain link to enclose a yard, a dog, or a work lot without paying for a solid screen. Gates and the coating change the price more than the footage slogan does.

Materials and styles

The estimate names the height, style, gate count, and anything on the line that changes the dig: slope, caliche, trees, and tear-out. We do not publish a flat price per foot.

Repair versus a new run

Sound posts can take a repair. Posts that have failed at the ground usually mean a new line is the clearer job. We will say which one it is after we see the fence.

What an estimate covers

Length, height, style, gates, post method, access, and removal of any existing fence that has to come out. Call (505) 210-9903 or use the form with the town and a rough foot count.

How a chain-link install goes

Terminal posts and tension do the work. The fabric is only as good as those posts.

  1. 1

    Confirm the use

    Dog run, backyard, or commercial yard. The use picks the height and how heavy the pipe should be.

  2. 2

    Choose the fabric

    Galvanized silver, or a black or green coating. The finish is part of the first estimate.

  3. 3

    Count the extras

    Gates, slats, and windscreen belong on the first estimate. They are not a small add-on after the fabric is stretched.

  4. 4

    Stretch and hang

    We set terminal posts, stretch the fabric, and hang gates so the gate is not what holds the tension in the line.

What changes a chain-link price

Length and gates are most of it. The ground and the finish are the rest.

Length

More fabric and more line posts. A long, open run is still simpler than a short run full of corners.

Gates

Each gate adds posts and hardware. A double drive gate is a different price from a walk gate.

Coating and height

Silver galvanized is the base. A color coating, and a taller fence, cost more.

Layout

Caliche, slope, and a yard the truck cannot reach change the labor even when the fabric price looks simple.

Chain-link questions

How does chain-link pricing work?

Most of the price is the length of the fence and the number of gates. Height, a color coating, slats, and a windscreen add to that. The ground still matters: caliche, slope, and access change the labor even on a simple fabric. We keep the estimate to those items instead of a single teaser rate.

Can I get black chain link instead of galvanized silver?

Yes. Standard fabric is zinc-coated steel, which reads as silver. Black or green usually means a coating over that steel. We price the finish you actually want, because the coated fabric is not the same line item as bare galvanized.

Will chain link keep a dog in the yard?

It can, if the height matches the dog and the bottom stays close to the ground. A determined digger will go under a fence that sits on soft soil. The gate latch matters as much as the fabric. Tell us the dog and we will price a height and a bottom that fit, without promising that every animal will stay put.

Can you add privacy slats or a windscreen?

Yes. Slats or a windscreen add screening that bare fabric does not have, and they also catch more wind. On an exposed lot that extra surface is part of the decision, not just a color choice. Say if you want them at the start so the posts and the price include them.

Is chain link only for commercial property?

No. It is a normal residential fence for a dog run, a side yard, or a backyard when you do not need privacy. It is also used on a commercial lot or a storage yard. The gauge, the height, and the fittings should match that use. A light backyard fence is not the spec we put on a commercial yard.

What is different about a windscreen on chain link?

A screen blocks more of the view and more of the dust, and it turns an open fence into something the wind can push. That is useful beside a court or a yard you want quieter, and it is a poor surprise on a line that already leans. We price the screen with the fabric, not after the posts are set.
