Fence Repair

Fence Repair in Albuquerque, NM

We repair fences when the posts are still doing their job. Boards, panels, chain-link fabric, and gates can be replaced on a sound line. A fence that leans because the posts have failed is usually a new run.

Request an estimate Call (505) 210-9903

When a repair is the right job

A repair fits broken boards, a stretched chain-link section, a gate that sags, or a few panels after a knock. The rest of the line can stay.

A repair is the wrong job when posts are soft, the fence leans for most of its length, or a wood line at the soil has rotted past the point of new boards. New material on failed posts follows the lean.

Where this fence fits local lots

Choose a repair when the damage is local and the posts are solid. Choose a new fence when the line leans or the posts are soft at the ground.

Materials and styles

The estimate names the height, style, gate count, and anything on the line that changes the dig: slope, caliche, trees, and tear-out. We do not publish a flat price per foot.

Repair versus a new run

Sound posts can take a repair. Posts that have failed at the ground usually mean a new line is the clearer job. We will say which one it is after we see the fence.

What an estimate covers

Length, height, style, gates, post method, access, and removal of any existing fence that has to come out. Call (505) 210-9903 or use the form with the town and a rough foot count.

How we look at a repair

The posts decide it. Everything else is material.

  1. 1

    See the damage

    Boards, fabric, a gate, or the whole line. A photo helps. The visit confirms it.

  2. 2

    Test the posts

    Solid posts can take new material. Soft, cracked, or leaning posts mean that section is a rebuild.

  3. 3

    Match what is there

    Board size, a vinyl profile, or chain-link height. A close match is not always the same product year.

  4. 4

    Fix and latch

    We replace what failed and check the gate. If the honest answer is replacement, we say that before we sell parts.

What changes a repair price

A small repair can still require a trip. The price is the damage, not a percentage of a new fence.

How much failed

A gate latch is not a hundred feet of new privacy fence. We price the section that needs work.

Posts or not

Reusing posts is the affordable repair. New posts in caliche are closer to an installation.

Matching material

A current vinyl profile or a board size that is still sold is straightforward. A discontinued panel can change the plan.

Access

A locked side yard or a line behind a pool still takes time, even when the broken part is small.

Fence repair questions

How much does fence repair cost?

It depends on what failed. A latch, a few boards, and a stretched run of chain link are different visits from a line that needs new posts. We do not quote repairs as a discount off a brand-new fence. Tell us the material and about how much of the line is damaged, and we will look before we name a number.

Can you repair a fence, or does the whole line have to come down?

The whole line comes down when the posts have failed or the lean runs most of the length. A local break, with solid posts on either side, can be repaired and the rest left alone. We would rather repair a sound fence than sell you a new one. We will not nail new boards to posts that will not hold them.

Can you fix a leaning fence?

If one section leans because a post shifted, that post can sometimes be reset or replaced. If the lean runs the length of the yard, the posts are the fence problem and new boards will lean with them. Wind and soft soil at the bottom are common reasons a line moves here. We look at how far the lean goes before we call it a repair.

Do you repair chain link, wood, vinyl, and gates?

Yes. Wood boards, vinyl panels when the profile can be matched, chain-link fabric and tension, and gates are the repairs we do. A vinyl panel that is no longer made may not have an exact match. Bring a photo of the broken part so we do not guess the product from the street.

Can you replace boards and leave the posts?

Yes, when the posts are still solid in the ground and not twisted out of line. That is the usual wood repair. If the posts are soft at the soil, especially where water sits, new boards will not save them. We check the posts with the boards, not after the new lumber is already on the truck as a full replacement.

When is a repair a waste of money?

When you would be paying to attach good material to posts that have already failed. It also wastes money to patch a fence you have already decided to replace with a different material next month. If vinyl is the real plan, we would rather price that install than stain a wood line you are about to remove.

Talk through the line

Call or send the lot details. We will tell you if the job is a repair, a replacement, or a different material than you started with.
