Fence Company in Los Ranchos, NM

Los Ranchos is a village in the North Valley, with larger lots than a city block and its own rules. Houses, horses, and ditches often sit on the same parcel, and they do not want the same fence.

What fence work looks like in Los Ranchos

A house yard may want wood or vinyl along a neighbor, while the rest of the acreage wants rail or field fence. Those are separate lines. A single price per foot will not describe both.

Irrigation ditches show up here the way they do in Corrales, but Los Ranchos is not Corrales. The village has its own code. Do not hand us a Corrales answer or a City of Albuquerque answer for a Los Ranchos parcel.

Access on a lot that is already fenced

Many of these properties already have a gate, a wall, or a ditch between the road and the new line. If panels have to be carried in, the labor is different from a lot the truck can drive. Tell us which one you have.

We do not decide the property line or the ditch easement. On a lot this size, a fence in the wrong place is a problem with a neighbor or with the water, not a style choice. Confirm both before we dig.

Questions about fencing in Los Ranchos

Can one Los Ranchos property have two kinds of fence?

Yes. Privacy along the house and rail or field fence on the longer boundary is a normal layout here. They use different posts and different materials, so they are separate lines on the estimate. Say where you want the look to change. We will not wrap the pasture in privacy panels to make the invoice simpler.

How are ditches handled on a North Valley fence?

We stop the fence where the ditch or its easement has to stay clear. You show us the water. We do not survey it and we do not pipe it. A gate may need to sit off the ditch bank so the swing stays dry. That placement is part of the walk-through.

Is Los Ranchos the same fence job as Corrales?

The animals and the ditches rhyme. The village does not. Los Ranchos has its own rules, and the lots sit closer into the valley than a west-mesa subdivision. Do not swap the two names on a permit question. Tell us Los Ranchos if that is the address.

Do you repair older wood fences in the village?

Yes, when the posts are solid. Boards and a gate can be replaced without a new line. If the posts are soft at the ground, new boards will lean with them. Older valley fences often fail at the soil, especially where irrigation keeps the bottom wet. We check that before we call it a repair.
