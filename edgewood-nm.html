Fence Company in Edgewood, NM

Edgewood is in the East Mountains, higher and windier than the river valley. Ranch lines and house lots share the area. A solid privacy fence that felt right in Albuquerque can be the wrong default on an exposed mountain lot.

What fence work looks like in Edgewood

Open aluminum, steel, and rail or field fence let wind through and fit longer lines. Vinyl or wood privacy still makes sense beside the house, where you want a screen and the run is short enough to build for the gusts.

This is not Corrales. Ditches are not the story. Wind, grade, and a longer drive from the valley are. The Town of Edgewood is not the City of Albuquerque, and a parcel outside town may be a different county office.

Winter and grade

The mountains get colder winters than the valley floor. That does not change the material list. It does mean a gate has to clear grade that heaves or slopes, and a fence built only for a flat Albuquerque pad will gap at the bottom.

Tell us the rough length and whether the line is a house yard or a ranch boundary. Those are different trips worth of material. We would rather hear that on the phone than arrive with privacy panels for a pasture.

Questions about fencing in Edgewood

Is a privacy fence a bad idea in Edgewood?

Not beside the house, if the posts and the panels are planned for an exposed site. It is a weak default around a whole mountain parcel, because the footage adds up and a solid wall catches wind. Use privacy where you want a screen. Use an open fence or a ranch line everywhere else.

Do you build ranch fence in the East Mountains?

Yes. Rail, horse fence, and field fence are the long-line products. The gate has to be wide enough for the truck that uses it, and the corners have to be braced. Tell us the animals. A horse and a cattle line are not interchangeable, at this elevation or any other.

Does the wind change how posts are set?

Yes. An exposed line needs posts set for that load, especially under a solid panel. An open fence sheds more of the gust and is often the better product in the open. We look at how much of the run is sheltered by the house or the terrain before we copy a valley layout onto the mountain.

Who do I ask about a fence permit in Edgewood?

Start with the Town of Edgewood if the parcel is inside town. Land outside town is not automatically that office, and it is not the City of Albuquerque. We do not print a height rule here. Ask the office for that parcel, and put Edgewood on the estimate so we are not planning a valley lot.
