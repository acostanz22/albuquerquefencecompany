Metal Fencing in Albuquerque, NM

Metal, on this page, means steel ornamental fence: heavier than aluminum, open rather than a privacy wall, and only as rust-resistant as its coating. It is not the same product as the aluminum fence.

When a steel fence is the right job

Choose steel ornamental fence for a front line, a commercial edge, or a yard that needs a stiffer picket than light aluminum. The look is open. You can still see through it.

Skip it when you need privacy, when you want the lowest-upkeep metal around a pool, or when the coating will be ignored. Dry air slows rust. It does not stop rust on steel that stays wet at the ground.

How a steel ornamental install goes

We name the metal on the estimate so aluminum and steel are not mixed into one word.

  1. 1

    Name the metal

    Steel, not aluminum, and the height and the picket style. A short decorative run and a taller commercial line are different weights.

  2. 2

    Check water at the ground

    Irrigation, a low spot, and splash off a wall are where coatings fail first. We look for that before we set posts.

  3. 3

    Match the gates

    The gate has to be the same family of metal and hardware. A light aluminum gate on a steel line is a mismatch we will not leave you with.

  4. 4

    Set and finish

    Posts, sections, and gates go in together. We look at the coating at cuts and hinges before we call it done.

What changes a steel fence price

Weight and the finish move the number. So do the gates.

Weight and height

A short decorative picket and a taller, heavier ornamental line do not use the same steel. Taller costs more.

Finish

A coated product and a bare or poorly coated one are not the same future. We price the finish you are actually buying.

Gates

Each leaf needs posts and hardware sized for that weight. A steel gate is not a light aluminum gate.

Ground

Caliche, slope, and a line that stays wet at the bottom change the labor and how we detail the bottoms.

Metal fence questions

How is a steel fence different from aluminum?

Steel is heavier and stiffer. Aluminum is lighter and does not rust the way steel does. Around a pool we usually steer you to aluminum. For a heavier ornamental line, steel is the conversation. The estimate names which metal, because a single word like metal hides the price.

Will steel rust in Albuquerque?

This is a dry climate, so steel rusts slower than it would where the air stays wet. It still rusts where a coating is cut, where a hinge rubs, or where irrigation keeps the bottom wet. The finish is part of the fence, not a decoration. We will not tell you steel is maintenance-free.

How much does ornamental metal fencing cost?

It is priced by the foot, then by the height, the weight of the section, and the gates. A short front-yard picket and a tall commercial ornamental line are not one rate. Ground that hits caliche changes the labor. We do not publish a dollar amount, because the line has to be seen.

Is wrought iron the same product as the steel you install?

People use wrought iron for this look. Most ornamental fence sold for yards today is steel formed to that look, then coated. We will say steel on the estimate if that is what the material is. We will not rename it to make a brochure sound older.

Can you match an existing metal fence?

Sometimes, if the picket, the height, and the post size are still made. Bring a photo and, if you can, a measurement of one section. An exact match is not a promise until we see the fence. A close match and a full replacement are different prices.

When is steel the wrong fence?

Skip it for a pool if you want the lower-rust choice, skip it when you need a solid screen, and skip it for horses. Steel ornamental fence is an open line. A pasture and a privacy yard are other pages.
