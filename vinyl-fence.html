Vinyl Fence

Vinyl Fencing in Albuquerque, NM

Vinyl is the privacy or picket fence for a yard you do not want to stain. We install it on homes in Albuquerque and the nearby towns, and we price it from the length, the style, and the gates.

Request an estimate Call (505) 210-9903

When vinyl is the right fence

Choose vinyl when you want privacy or a picket line and you do not want a fence that needs stain. It is a common replacement for wood that has gone gray in the sun.

It is a weaker choice on a long pasture, where rail or wire costs less per foot, and where you want a fence you can restain a new color every few years.

Where this fence fits local lots

Choose vinyl for a privacy or picket fence you do not want to stain. A typical privacy panel looks finished from both sides, which helps on a shared lot line.

Materials and styles

The estimate names the height, style, gate count, and anything on the line that changes the dig: slope, caliche, trees, and tear-out. We do not publish a flat price per foot.

Repair versus a new run

Sound posts can take a repair. Posts that have failed at the ground usually mean a new line is the clearer job. We will say which one it is after we see the fence.

What an estimate covers

Length, height, style, gates, post method, access, and removal of any existing fence that has to come out. Call (505) 210-9903 or use the form with the town and a rough foot count.

How a vinyl install goes

The visit decides the style and the steps. The build itself is posts, panels, and gates.

  1. 1

    Name the style

    Privacy, picket, or ranch rail, plus the height and the color. Ranch rail is a two-, three-, or four-rail perimeter fence, not a screen.

  2. 2

    Walk the line

    We look at the slope, the gate openings, and whether panels need to step. A flat catalog panel will not follow a drop by itself.

  3. 3

    Price the whole run

    Footage, gates, and any wood fence that has to come out are on the estimate before we dig. Caliche in the hole is labor, not a surprise after the holes are started.

  4. 4

    Set posts and hang panels

    Posts go in, panels go up, and gates get hung so they latch. We check the bottoms against the grade before we leave.

What changes a vinyl price

Vinyl usually costs more to install than a basic wood privacy fence. These are the items that move the number.

Style and height

A solid privacy panel, a picket, and a ranch rail do not use the same amount of material. Taller privacy costs more than a low picket.

Gates

Every gate is hardware, a post pair, and time. A wider gate costs more than a single walk gate.

Grade and ground

Stepped panels take longer than a level run. Holes that hit caliche take longer than holes in loose fill.

Tear-out and access

Hauling off an old wood fence, or carrying panels through a narrow side yard, is labor the footage alone does not show.

Vinyl fence questions

How much more does vinyl cost than wood?

Vinyl usually costs more to install than a basic wood privacy fence of the same length. Wood stain and board repairs over the years are the costs vinyl is meant to avoid. The gap depends on the style, the gates, and how the ground digs. We price both from the same layout so you can compare one job.

Will vinyl fade in the New Mexico sun?

Strong sun can fade a vinyl fence, especially a dark color. That is not the same as rot, and it is not a promise that the color will look new in ten years. Dirt can still be washed off. If color stability matters, say so before we price a dark panel against a light one.

Does a vinyl privacy fence have a good side and a bad side?

Many vinyl privacy panels look finished from both yards, with no exposed rail side. That helps when the fence sits on a shared line and neither neighbor wants the frame. Picket and ranch rail are open styles, so there is no solid face to assign. Ask which profile you are pricing.

Can vinyl privacy panels handle the wind here?

A solid panel catches more wind than an open picket or a chain-link fence. Post depth and how the panels are tied to the posts matter more on a windy mesa lot than on a sheltered backyard. We look at how exposed the line is before we treat it like a calm courtyard. Wind is a layout fact, not a slogan.

Do you install vinyl picket and ranch rail, or only privacy panels?

We install privacy panels, picket, and ranch rail. Ranch rail comes in two, three, or four rails and does not screen the yard the way a privacy panel does. Picket is the open front-yard look, and privacy is the solid backyard fence. Name the style when you call so the estimate matches it.

When should I skip vinyl?

Skip it on a long pasture, when you want to change the color with stain later, or when the job is a commercial chain-link spec. Vinyl is a yard fence. It is not the default for horses, a storage yard, or a pool that needs an open view of the water.
