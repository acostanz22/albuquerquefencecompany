Fence Service Areas

We install fences in Albuquerque and the towns around it. The pages below are about the fence work in each place, not a town history.

Request an estimate Call (505) 210-9903

Albuquerque, NM

City lots often already have a block wall, so the fence job is a gate, a wood or vinyl run, or chain link. Caliche and sun show up in the holes and on bare wood.

Fence details

Rio Rancho, NM

West mesa lots deal with wind, rock, and subdivision rules. Vinyl and masonry-adjacent fences are common. A city backyard price from Albuquerque is the wrong assumption.

Fence details

Corrales, NM

Acreage, horses, and irrigation ditches. Privacy fence belongs near the house. The long line is usually rail or field fence, and the ditch comes first.

Fence details

Los Ranchos, NM

North Valley lots are larger than city blocks and often include a ditch. House privacy and a horse line can share one property without sharing one fence.

Fence details

Bernalillo, NM

The Town of Bernalillo is not Bernalillo County. In-town yards and the acreage at the edge are different fences, and Albuquerque rules do not cover either one.

Fence details

Los Lunas, NM

Valencia County valley lots mix newer yards with irrigation and livestock at the edges. The fence follows the animals and the water, not an Albuquerque subdivision.

Fence details

Edgewood, NM

East Mountain wind and longer ranch lines. A solid privacy fence catches weather that an open fence lets through. This is not a valley ditch job.

Fence details

Neighborhoods are not separate fence types

A yard in the Northeast Heights and a yard on the West Side ask for the same thing on install day: posts that deal with the ground, and a gate that still swings. Naming every neighborhood would not change the material or the price method.

What does change is the lot and the locality. City yards are tighter and often already have a block wall. Rio Rancho sits on the mesa. Corrales and Los Ranchos bring ditches and horses. Los Lunas is the valley south of the city. Edgewood is wind and ranch lines. The Town of Bernalillo is not Bernalillo County. Start with the place page, then the service that matches the job.
