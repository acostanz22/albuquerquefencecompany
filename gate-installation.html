Gate Installation

Gate Installation in Albuquerque, NM

A gate is the part of the fence you use every day. We install and repair walk gates and drive gates so they latch, clear the ground, and match the fence they interrupt.

Request an estimate Call (505) 210-9903

When the job is the gate

A gate is its own job when it sags, when it will not latch, or when you need a new opening in a fence we are already building. The posts have to be sized for the leaf.

Many yards here already have a block wall. If the opening is already there, the gate has to fit that opening. We hang fence gates. We do not build the block wall.

Where this fence fits local lots

Choose a new gate when the opening is wrong, or a repair when the leaf and the posts are still sound and the hardware is the problem.

Materials and styles

The estimate names the height, style, gate count, and anything on the line that changes the dig: slope, caliche, trees, and tear-out. We do not publish a flat price per foot.

Repair versus a new run

Sound posts can take a repair. Posts that have failed at the ground usually mean a new line is the clearer job. We will say which one it is after we see the fence.

What an estimate covers

Length, height, style, gates, post method, access, and removal of any existing fence that has to come out. Call (505) 210-9903 or use the form with the town and a rough foot count.

How a gate job goes

We start with the opening, then the swing, then the hardware.

  1. 1

    Measure the opening

    Width, height, and what the gate mounts to: wood posts, steel posts, or an existing masonry opening.

  2. 2

    Decide the swing

    In or out, and whether the grade rises into the swing. A gate that scrapes will not get better on its own.

  3. 3

    Match the fence

    Wood, vinyl, chain link, aluminum, or steel. The leaf should belong to the fence beside it.

  4. 4

    Hang and latch

    Hinges, a latch, and a drop rod on a double gate. We open and close it before we leave.

What changes a gate price

A gate is hardware and posts, not a leftover panel.

Width

A walk gate and a drive gate are different leaves, different posts, and different hardware.

Material

A vinyl privacy gate, a chain-link gate, and a steel ornamental gate do not share a price.

The opening

New posts in soil are a different job from a leaf that has to fit an existing block opening.

Repair versus new

New hinges on sound posts can be a repair. Posts that have racked usually mean a new opening.

Gate questions

How much does a gate cost?

A gate is priced from the width, the material, and whether we are setting new posts or using an opening that already exists. One walk gate on a new fence is a line on that estimate. A double drive gate is a different job. We do not fold it into a flat rate per foot of fence.

Can you repair a gate that will not latch?

Often, if the posts are still plumb and the leaf is not twisted. Hinges, a latch, and a drag on the ground are repair items. If the posts have leaned, adjusting the latch only hides it until the next season. We look at the posts before we call it a hardware visit.

Can you hang a gate in an existing block wall?

If the opening is already there, we can price a gate that fits it, as long as the jambs can take the hardware. We do not cut a new opening in a block wall, and we do not build the wall. Measure the clear opening and tell us what the gate mounts to. That is the estimate.

Do you install drive gates as well as walk gates?

Yes. A drive gate is wider, heavier, and needs posts and hardware sized for that leaf. A double gate needs a drop rod and a meeting point that stays aligned. Tell us what has to pass through: a mower, a car, or a truck. The width comes from that, not from a default.

Do you install gate motors?

This page is the gate itself: the leaf, the posts, and the latch. If you also want an operator, say so when you call. We will tell you whether that belongs on the same visit or whether the gate has to be right before anyone talks about a motor. We will not add a motor to an estimate you did not ask for.

What makes a gate sag?

The leaf is heavier than a fence panel, and undersized posts or loose hinges let it drop until it scrapes. Grade that rises into the swing does the same thing on a gate that was built for flat ground. A sagging gate is usually a post or a hinge problem. New boards on the leaf will not lift it.

Talk through the line

Call or send the lot details. We will tell you if the job is a repair, a replacement, or a different material than you started with.
