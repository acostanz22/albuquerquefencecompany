About Albuquerque Fence Company

We install and repair residential, commercial, and farm fences in Albuquerque and the nearby towns. The work happens at the property. This site does not list a showroom address.

Request an estimate Call (505) 210-9903

What we install

Vinyl, wood, chain link, aluminum, steel ornamental fence, picket, farm fence, gates, and commercial fencing. If you need a horse fence, a pool fence, or a wood privacy fence repaired, start with the matching service page or call (505) 210-9903.

An estimate starts with the line: approximate length, material, gates, the ground, and access. We do not publish a price per foot, because a level yard and a caliche hole are not the same job.

How an estimate works

Call or use the form. Estimates are free. Say whether a fence is already there and whether you want it repaired or taken out. If the posts are the problem, we will say so instead of selling boards onto a bad line.

We cover Albuquerque, Rio Rancho, Corrales, Los Ranchos, Bernalillo, Los Lunas, and Edgewood. If the property is farther out, say so on the form and we will tell you whether we can take the trip.

This page does not list a year the company opened, a license number, or a warranty term. Those details are not published here. What we will do is price the fence in front of us and build that fence.
