Fence Company in Corrales, NM

Corrales is a village of longer lots, horses, and irrigation ditches between Albuquerque and Rio Rancho. A privacy fence around the whole parcel is usually the wrong product.

Request an estimate Call (505) 210-9903

What fence work looks like in Corrales

The long boundary is where farm fence belongs: horse rail the animal can see, or field fence where the animals are different. Wood or vinyl privacy fits beside the house, not around every acre.

An acequia or ditch on the line is a layout problem before it is a style problem. We do not run posts through a ditch to keep a catalog line straight. The Village of Corrales is its own office, not the City of Albuquerque and not Rio Rancho.

Gates have to fit the equipment

A pasture gate has to clear a truck or a trailer. Tell us the width before we price hinge posts. A light line post will not carry that leaf, and on a Corrales lot the gate is often a large part of the job.

House yards still need a person-sized gate and, sometimes, a place for a mower. Those are separate from the farm gate. If you want both, they are two lines on the estimate.

Questions about fencing in Corrales

Do you build horse fence in Corrales?

Yes. Horses need a fence they can see, so rail or a rail-and-mesh mix is the usual request, not a solid privacy wall around the pasture. Tell us how the horses are kept and where the trailer has to pass. Barbed wire alone is a poor horse fence, and we will not price it as one.

Can the fence cross an irrigation ditch?

Not if that blocks the ditch or ignores the easement. Show us the water before we draw a straight line on a photo. Posts and gates have to work around it. This comes up in Corrales more than it does on a Rio Rancho subdivision lot, and it changes the layout.

Should the whole property be a privacy fence?

Usually no. The footage on a village lot makes privacy panels expensive, and a solid line catches wind. Screen the part by the house or the neighbor. Leave the long boundary as rail or field fence. We price those as different products because they are different products.

Are Corrales fence rules the same as Albuquerque?

No. The Village of Corrales handles its own parcels. Do not use a City of Albuquerque answer, and do not use a City of Rio Rancho answer, just because the village sits between them. Ask the village about height and placement on that lot before we dig.
