Houses, businesses, and acreage

Homes

A lot of Albuquerque yards already have a block wall. The fence job is often a gate in that opening, a wood or vinyl run where the wall stops, or chain link for a dog.

A repair is the right job when the posts are still sound. When the posts have failed at the ground, replacing the run is usually clearer than patching boards onto a lean.

Businesses and acreage

Commercial lines are chain link, ornamental metal, and gates for lots, storage yards, and job sites. The pipe and the openings are specified for that traffic.

Farm lines are longer: horse rail and field fence, with a gate a truck can use. In the valley, a ditch on the line is part of the layout. Those are different products from a privacy panel.

What we ask before we price a fence

These are the facts that decide the estimate. We would rather ask them up front than invent a rate per foot.

How many feet

The length is the first number. A rough measurement is enough to start, as long as it includes the corners.

The ground

Caliche, rock, slope, a ditch, and whether the truck can reach the line. Two of those can matter more than the style.

Style and gates

Privacy, picket, chain link, aluminum, steel, or farm fence, plus every gate. The style is part of the price.

Repair or replace

Sound posts can take a repair. A line that leans, or posts that are soft at the soil, is usually a new run.

Fence questions

Short answers. Material-specific questions live on each service page, and the full set is on the FAQ.

How much does a fence cost in Albuquerque?

A fence is priced by the foot, and that foot price changes with the material, the height, and the style. The same length costs more when posts hit caliche, when the line is hard for a truck to reach, or when you add gates. We do not publish a flat rate. A level vinyl run and a long horse fence beside a ditch are different jobs, so the number comes after we see the layout.

Can you repair a fence, or do you only install new ones?

We repair fences and we install new ones. A repair makes sense when the posts are still sound and the damage is a few boards, a gate, or a stretched section of chain link. If the posts are soft at the ground or the line leans for most of its length, replacement is usually the clearer job. We will tell you which one it is after we see the fence.

Do you offer wood, vinyl, chain link, aluminum, and farm fencing?

Yes. House jobs are usually vinyl or wood privacy, picket, chain link, aluminum, or a heavier steel ornamental fence. Longer properties get rail, horse fence, and field fence, especially where the lot is measured in acres instead of a backyard. Commercial jobs use chain link, metal, and gates. Each of those has its own page so you can see what the build includes.

Do I need a permit to put up a fence in Albuquerque?

The City of Albuquerque, the City of Rio Rancho, the villages, and the counties do not share one fence rule. Height, a front yard, a corner lot, and a pool can change what that office allows. We do not print a permit threshold on this site, because the parcel decides it. Name the place when you request the estimate, and check with the office that covers the property before posts go in.

Can you install a fence in caliche or on a windy lot?

Yes. A lot of holes in this basin hit caliche, which is harder than ordinary soil and slower to dig. Wind is the other local fact: a solid privacy fence catches it, and an open aluminum or chain-link fence lets more of it through. The layout is the second thing we ask about after the length, because it changes post depth, how the fence steps, and whether a gate will swing.
