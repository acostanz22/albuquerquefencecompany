Fence Company in Albuquerque, NM

Albuquerque Fence Company installs and repairs fences in the city. A lot of yards already have a block wall, so the work is often a gate, a wood or vinyl section, or chain link, not a new fence around every property line.

What fence work looks like in Albuquerque

Older and newer neighborhoods both use masonry walls. Where the wall stops, people add wood, vinyl, or chain link. A gate in an opening that already exists is its own job. We do not build the block.

Post holes often hit caliche. Bare wood grays in the sun. A solid privacy fence catches spring wind more than an open aluminum line. Those are pricing facts, not scenery.

City limits and the South Valley are not the same office

A parcel inside the City of Albuquerque is not automatically under the same fence rules as a lot in unincorporated Bernalillo County, including parts of the South Valley. Height, a front yard, and a pool are questions for the office that covers that parcel. We do not print a single height on this site.

Neighborhood names do not change the product. A tight yard in the Northeast Heights and a tight yard on the West Side still come down to length, access, gates, and the ground. The North Valley is different when the lot gets longer and a ditch shows up, which is why Los Ranchos has its own page.

Questions about fencing in Albuquerque

Do you only replace fences, or can you add a gate to a block wall?

Both, within a limit. We install and repair fences, and we hang a gate when the opening is already there and the jambs can take the hardware. We do not cut new openings in block or build the wall. If the wall ends and you need wood, vinyl, or chain link from there, that is a fence line we can price.

Why do post holes in Albuquerque take longer?

Caliche is common in this basin. It is a hard layer, and an auger that sails through fill will stop in it. That labor belongs on the estimate after we see the line, not as a flat rate copied from a soft-soil city. A short fence with hard holes can take more time than a longer fence in easy digging.

Does a wood fence make sense in this sun?

Yes, if someone will stain it or if you want wood anyway. Bare boards gray quickly here. The soil line still rots when irrigation or wall splash keeps it wet, even though the air is dry. If you do not want that upkeep, price vinyl for the same layout and compare the two numbers.

Are South Valley lots priced as city lots?

The pricing method is the same: length, material, gates, and ground. The rules may not be. Parts of the South Valley are Bernalillo County rather than the City of Albuquerque. Tell us the actual place so we are not using a city answer on a county parcel.
