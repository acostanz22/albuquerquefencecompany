Match the fence to the job

Use this to pick a page. The price still depends on the line at the property.

Vinyl Fence

Choose vinyl for a privacy or picket fence you do not want to stain. A typical privacy panel looks finished from both sides, which helps on a shared lot line.

Wood Fence

Choose wood when you want privacy, picket, or dog-ear boards you can stain. Plan on upkeep, especially where sun and irrigation water meet the boards.

Chain-link Fence

Choose chain link to enclose a yard, a dog, or a work lot without paying for a solid screen. Gates and the coating change the price more than the footage slogan does.

Aluminum Fence

Choose aluminum when the fence should stay open, including around a pool. Sections can follow a slope more easily than a tall privacy panel.

Metal Fence

Choose steel when you want an open ornamental line that is heavier than aluminum. Plan on the finish, because bare steel will rust where water sits.

Picket Fence

Choose picket when the yard should stay open. Wood can be painted or stained. Vinyl skips that coat. Neither one is a privacy wall.

Farm Fence

Choose farm fence when the line is long and the point is animals or a boundary, not a screen. The species and the gates decide the material.

Gate Installation

Choose a new gate when the opening is wrong, or a repair when the leaf and the posts are still sound and the hardware is the problem.

Commercial Fencing

Choose a commercial spec for a lot, a storage yard, or a job site. Height, gauge, and the gates follow that use, not a house catalog.

Fence Repair

Choose a repair when the damage is local and the posts are solid. Choose a new fence when the line leans or the posts are soft at the ground.
