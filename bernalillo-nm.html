Fence Company in Bernalillo, NM

Bernalillo, the town, sits north of Albuquerque in Sandoval County. In-town yards and the acreage at the edge are different fences. The town is not Bernalillo County, which is the county Albuquerque sits in.

What fence work looks like in the town

In-town lots usually want a wood or vinyl backyard fence, picket or aluminum where the yard should stay open, and a gate that fits the side yard. The footage is a house lot, not a pasture.

Toward the river and the edges, the line gets longer and farm fence starts to make sense. We will not price a town backyard and a field as the same product because they share a zip code.

Do not use the county name as the town

If the property is in the Town of Bernalillo, say the town. If it is somewhere else in Bernalillo County, such as Albuquerque or the South Valley, say that instead. The offices are different, and a swapped name sends the permit question to the wrong place.

Sandoval County covers land outside the town limits. The City of Albuquerque does not cover the town. Put the real place on the estimate so the height and the placement are planned for that parcel.

Questions about fencing in Bernalillo

Is a fence in the Town of Bernalillo a Bernalillo County job?

The town is inside Sandoval County. Bernalillo County is a different county, and it is the one that contains Albuquerque. A fence permit question for a house in the town does not go to Bernalillo County. Use the town's name when you call us so we do not plan the job for the wrong office.

Do you fence both town lots and acreage at the edge?

Yes. A town backyard is usually wood, vinyl, chain link, or an open aluminum line, plus a gate. Acreage at the edge is more often rail or field fence and a gate a truck can use. Tell us which property you mean, because the drive is similar and the fence is not.

Can you repair a fence in town without replacing it?

Yes, when the posts are sound. Boards, a chain-link section, or a gate can be repaired on their own. A line that leans for most of a small yard is often a replacement, because there is not much sound fence left to save. We look before we choose.

Do Albuquerque fence rules apply in Bernalillo?

No. The Town of Bernalillo is not the City of Albuquerque. Height and front-yard placement are questions for the town, or for Sandoval County if the parcel is outside town limits. We do not copy an Albuquerque answer onto this address.
