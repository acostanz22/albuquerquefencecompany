Fence Company in Los Lunas, NM

Los Lunas is in Valencia County, south of Albuquerque in the river valley. Newer yards and older agricultural edges sit near each other. Irrigation and livestock change the fence in a way a northeast Albuquerque lot usually does not.

What fence work looks like in Los Lunas

Subdivision yards ask for vinyl, wood, or chain link and a couple of gates. The edge of town asks for field fence or horse rail and a gate sized for a truck.

Irrigation water on the line is a layout fact. We do not set a wood privacy fence in a wet ditch bank and call it the same job as a dry side yard. Valencia County and the Village of Los Lunas are not the City of Albuquerque.

South of the metro is still a different parcel

The drive from Albuquerque does not make the rules the same. A lot inside the Village of Los Lunas and a lot in unincorporated Valencia County can be different offices. Name which one so the estimate is for that place.

Bring a rough length, the material, and whether animals or a ditch are on the line. A photo of a neighbor's fence helps only if you want that fence. It does not tell us what is under the ground.

Questions about fencing in Los Lunas

Do you install fences in Los Lunas subdivision yards?

Yes. Those jobs are usually vinyl, wood, or chain link, with a walk gate and sometimes a wider gate for a mower. They are priced like house lots: footage, gates, and access. They are not priced like a pasture just because Los Lunas is south of the city.

Can you fence property with livestock south of town?

Yes. Horses, cattle, and goats need different fence, and the gate has to fit the trailer or the truck. Tell us the animals before we choose rail, field fence, or a tighter mesh. A privacy quote will not hold livestock, and we will not pretend it will.

How do irrigation ditches affect the price?

They change where posts can go and how much of the line stays wet. Wet ground is a bad place for a wood bottom to live, and it is a bad place to block water. If a ditch crosses the run, show us on the visit. The layout may be shorter or bent compared with the property line you first measured.

Are Los Lunas permits handled by Albuquerque?

No. Los Lunas is in Valencia County. Start with the Village of Los Lunas if the lot is in the village, and with the county if it is not. An Albuquerque fence answer does not travel south with the crew.
